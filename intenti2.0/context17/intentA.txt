The logistics provider deliveryLogisticsOp requires a route optimization service to manage 15,000 couriers. This software receives the GPS positions of a fleet, 
analyzes city traffic data, constantly recalculates the best route for each courier, and updates their terminals. The deployed service chain includes, in order: 
a gps ingester allocated at the edge, a traffic analyzer located in the cloud, a route optimizer located in the cloud, and a terminal updater allocated at the edge. 
Regarding requirements, compression is applied after the gps ingester. Furthermore, decoding is executed before the terminal updater. Finally, it is strictly required 
that the latency from the begin to the end of the chain remains below 50 ms.
