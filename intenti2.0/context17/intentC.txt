The logistics provider deliveryLogisticsOp requires a route optimization service to manage 15,000 couriers. This software receives the GPS positions of a fleet, 
analyzes city traffic data, constantly recalculates the best route for each courier, and updates their terminals. Specifying that compression is applied after the 
initial telemetry receiver, the service begins with a gps ingester allocated at the edge connected to a traffic analyzer located in the cloud and a route optimizer 
located in the cloud. Ensuring that decoding is executed before the final synchronization engine, the system incorporates a terminal updater allocated at the edge. 
Finally, strictly requiring that the latency from the begin to the end of the chain remains below 50 ms, the system completes the optimization pipeline.
