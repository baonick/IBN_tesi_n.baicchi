The media provider liveSubsOp requires a live subtitling service to manage 10,000 concurrent streams. This service intercepts the audio of a 
streaming event, transforms it into text via AI, translates it into three languages, and reinserts it into the video feed as subtitles. 
The deployed service chain includes, in order: an audio extractor allocated at the edge, a speech to text function located in the cloud, 
a multi translator located in the cloud, and a subtitle injector allocated at the edge. Regarding requirements, caching is applied after 
the audio extractor. Furthermore, the provider prefers that the total chain hardware remains lower than 100 gb. Finally, it is strictly 
required that the latency from the speech to text function to the subtitle injector remains below 20 ms.
