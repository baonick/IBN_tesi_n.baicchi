The media provider liveSubsOp requires a live subtitling service to manage 10,000 concurrent streams. This service intercepts the audio 
of a streaming event, transforms it into text via AI, translates it into three languages, and reinserts it into the video feed as subtitles. 
Specifying that caching is applied after the initial extraction function, the service begins with an audio extractor allocated at the edge 
connected to a speech to text function located in the cloud and a multi translator located in the cloud. Preferring that the total chain 
hardware is lower than 100 gb, and strictly requiring that the latency from the AI transcription phase to the final injection engine remains 
below 20 ms, the system completes the pipeline with a subtitle injector allocated at the edge.
