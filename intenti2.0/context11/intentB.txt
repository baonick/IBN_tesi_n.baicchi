The media provider liveSubsOp requires a live subtitling service to manage 10,000 concurrent streams. This service intercepts the audio of a 
streaming event, transforms it into text via AI, translates it into three languages, and reinserts it into the video feed as subtitles. 
The service chain begins with an audio extractor allocated at the edge. For this component, caching is applied after it. The chain then 
connects to a speech to text function located in the cloud followed by a multi translator located in the cloud. For the entire deployment, 
it is ideally preferred that the total chain hardware remains lower than 100 gb. Finally, the text is routed to a subtitle injector allocated 
at the edge, ensuring that the latency from the speech to text function to this subtitle injector must remain below 10 ms.
