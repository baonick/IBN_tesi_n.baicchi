The enterprise provider corpDocOp requires a document classification engine to manage 50,000 daily files. This system analyzes PDF files or 
OCR scans, identifies the document type like invoice, contract, or reminder, and routes it to the competent business function. 
The deployed service chain includes, in order: an ocr scanner allocated at the edge, a text extractor located in the edge, an ai 
classifier located in the cloud, a data redactor located in the cloud, and a document router located in the cloud. Regarding requirements, 
security is applied after the ocr scanner. Furthermore, it is a mandatory requirement that the ocr scanner and the text extractor are deployed 
on the same hosting node. It is strictly required that the latency from the text extractor to the ai classifier remains below 50 ms. 
Additionally, privacy is executed before the data redactor. Finally, the provider prefers that the bandwidth from the ai classifier to 
the document router remains higher than 500 mbps.
