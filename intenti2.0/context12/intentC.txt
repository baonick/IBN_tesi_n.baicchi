The enterprise provider corpDocOp requires a document classification engine to manage 50,000 daily files. This system analyzes PDF 
files or OCR scans, identifies the document type like invoice, contract, or reminder, and routes it to the competent business function. 
Specifying that security is applied after the initial scanning phase, and mandating that this first component and the subsequent extraction 
service are deployed on the same hosting node, the service begins with an ocr scanner allocated at the edge connected to a text extractor 
located in the edge. Strictly requiring that the latency from the extraction service to the categorization engine remains below 50 ms, the 
chain incorporates an ai classifier located in the cloud. Ensuring that privacy is executed before the masking service, the chain connects 
to a data redactor located in the cloud. Finally, preferring a bandwidth higher than 500 mbps between the categorization engine and the final 
distribution point, the system completes the pipeline with a document router located in the cloud.
