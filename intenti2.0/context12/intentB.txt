The enterprise provider corpDocOp requires a document classification engine to manage 50,000 daily files. This system analyzes PDF 
files or OCR scans, identifies the document type like invoice, contract, or reminder, and routes it to the competent business function. 
The service chain begins with an ocr scanner allocated at the edge. For this component, security is applied after it. It is mandatory that 
this ocr scanner and the next text extractor, located in the edge, are deployed on the same hosting node. The chain then connects to an ai 
classifier located in the cloud; for this connection, it is strictly required that the latency from the text extractor to the ai classifier 
remains below 50 ms. The chain proceeds to a data redactor located in the cloud, ensuring that privacy is executed before it. Finally, the 
files are routed to a document router located in the cloud, and it is ideally preferred that the bandwidth from the ai classifier to this 
document router remains higher than 500 mbps.
