The government provider govMessagingOp requires a service to manage 50,000 institutional messages. This service receives digital communications, 
verifies the electronic signature of the sender, checks its integrity, and archives the message in a legal vault. Specifying that caching is 
applied after the first function, the service begins with a message receiver allocated at the edge. Mandating that the subsequent verification 
service and the integrity checker are deployed on the same hosting node, the chain connects to a signature verifier located in the cloud and 
an integrity checker located in the cloud. Preferring that the total chain hardware is lower than 100 gb, and ensuring that compression is 
executed before the final archiving vault, the system completes the pipeline with a legal vault located in the cloud.
