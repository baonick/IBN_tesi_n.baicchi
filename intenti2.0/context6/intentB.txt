The government provider govMessagingOp requires a service to manage 50,000 institutional messages. This service receives digital communications, 
verifies the electronic signature of the sender, checks its integrity, and archives the message in a legal vault. The service chain begins with 
a message receiver allocated at the edge. For this component, caching is applied after it. The chain connects to a signature verifier located in 
the cloud. It is strictly required that this signature verifier and the next component, an integrity checker located in the cloud, are deployed 
on the same hosting node. For the entire deployment, it is ideally preferred that the total chain hardware remains lower than 100 gb. Finally, 
the messages are routed to a legal vault located in the cloud, ensuring that compression is executed before it.
