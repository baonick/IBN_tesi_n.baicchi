The government provider govMessagingOp requires a service to manage 50,000 institutional messages. This service receives digital communications,
verifies the electronic signature of the sender, checks its integrity, and archives the message in a legal vault. The deployed service chain 
includes, in order: a message receiver allocated at the edge, a signature verifier located in the cloud, an integrity checker located in the 
cloud, and a legal vault located in the cloud. Regarding requirements, caching is applied after the message receiver. Furthermore, the provider 
prefers to keep the total chain hardware lower than 100 gb. It is a mandatory requirement that the signature verifier and the integrity checker 
operate on the same node. Finally, compression is applied before the legal vault.
