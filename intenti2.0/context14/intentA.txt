The financial provider hftGlobalOp requires a high frequency trading service to manage 500,000 orders. This infrastructure receives stock prices, 
executes decision algorithms, and sends purchase orders seeking the fastest network path. The deployed service chain includes, in order: a market 
feed allocated at the edge, a trading algo allocated at the edge, a risk checker located in the cloud, and an order dispatcher allocated at the edge. 
Regarding requirements, caching is applied after the market feed. Furthermore, it is strictly required that the latency from the market feed to the 
trading algo remains below 2 ms. The provider prefers that the bandwidth from the trading algo to the risk checker remains higher than 5000 mbps. 
Additionally, it is desired that the latency from the risk checker to the order dispatcher remains below 5 ms. Finally, logging is executed before 
the order dispatcher.
