The financial provider hftGlobalOp requires a high frequency trading service to manage 500,000 orders. This infrastructure receives stock prices, 
executes decision algorithms, and sends purchase orders seeking the fastest network path. Specifying that caching is applied after the initial ingestion 
function, the service begins with a market feed allocated at the edge. Mandating that the latency from this first component to the decision engine remains 
below 2 ms, the chain connects to a trading algo allocated at the edge. Preferring that the bandwidth from the decision engine to the validation layer remains 
higher than 5000 mbps, the chain incorporates a risk checker located in the cloud. Preferring that the latency from the validation layer to the final execution 
phase remains below 5 ms, and ensuring that logging is executed before this final component, the system completes the pipeline with an order dispatcher allocated 
at the edge.
