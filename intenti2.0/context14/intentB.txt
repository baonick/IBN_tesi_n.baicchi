The financial provider hftGlobalOp requires a high frequency trading service to manage 500,000 orders. This infrastructure receives stock prices, 
executes decision algorithms, and sends purchase orders seeking the fastest network path. The service chain begins with a market feed allocated at the edge. 
For this component, caching is applied after it. The chain then connects to a trading algo allocated at the edge, ensuring that it is mandatory for the latency 
from the market feed to this trading algo to remain below 2 ms. The chain proceeds to a risk checker located in the cloud; it is ideally preferred that the 
bandwidth from the trading algo to this risk checker remains higher than 5000 mbps. Finally, the system routes to an order dispatcher allocated at the edge, 
with a preference that the latency from the risk checker to this order dispatcher remains below 5 ms, and ensuring that logging is executed before it.
