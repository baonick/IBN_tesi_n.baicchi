The service provider eInvoiceOp requires a digital invoicing service to manage 50,000 invoices per day. This system transforms sales data into certified XML files, 
applies the digital signature, and automatically sends them to governmental systems for fiscal validation. Mandating that the total chain hardware remains lower 
than 150 gb, the service begins with a sale data collector allocated at the edge connected to an xml converter located in the cloud. Strictly requiring that the 
latency from the conversion engine to the signing service remains below 30 ms, the chain incorporates a digital signer located in the cloud. Finally, preferring 
that the bandwidth from the signing engine to the final validation point remains higher than 100 mbps, the system completes the pipeline with a tax authority 
transmitter located in the cloud.
