The IT provider corpITOp requires a service to manage 5,000 office employees. This service must filter dangerous websites, 
scan downloaded files for malware, and ensure that sensitive data does not leave the network. The service chain includes, 
in order: a web filter allocated at the edge, a malware scanner located in the cloud, and a dlp engine located in the cloud. 
Regarding requirements, it is a mandatory requirement that the latency from the web filter to the malware scanner remains below 10 milliseconds. 
Furthermore, the bandwidth between the malware scanner and the dlp engine must be greater than 500 mbps.
