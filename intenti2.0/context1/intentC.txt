The IT provider corpITOp requires a service to manage 5,000 office employees. This service must filter dangerous websites, 
scan downloaded files for malware, and ensure that sensitive data does not leave the network. Regarding requirements, 
ensuring a mandatory latency below 10 milliseconds, the service chain establishes a connection between a web filter allocated at the edge 
and a malware scanner located in the cloud. Following this, guaranteeing a bandwidth greater than 500 mbps, the service proceeds to connect 
the malware scanner to a dlp engine located in the cloud.
