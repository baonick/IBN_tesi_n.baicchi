The financial provider onlineBankOp requires a digital identity verification service to manage 10,000 daily uploads. This service processes photos of 
documents uploaded by users, verifies their authenticity, and compares the data with international blacklists to prevent money laundering. 
The deployed service chain includes, in order: a document receiver allocated at the edge, an authenticity checker located in the cloud, an aml matcher 
located in the cloud, and a profile creator located in the cloud. Regarding requirements, encoding is applied after the document receiver. Security is 
executed before the authenticity checker. Privacy is applied after the aml matcher. Finally, compression is executed before the profile creator.
