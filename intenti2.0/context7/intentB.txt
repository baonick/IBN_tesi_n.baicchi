Cloud gaming service provider GamingOp needs a service to handle 40,000 active players. This service runs games on powerful servers and 
streams the video to the user, ensuring very low latency to prevent the game from becoming unplayable. The service chain begins with a 
game executor hosted in the cloud. Encoding is applied to this component. Ideally, this game executor is preferred to run on a dedicated node. 
The chain then connects to a streaming packager hosted in the cloud; this component is also preferred to run on a dedicated node. Finally, 
the traffic is routed to a distribution optimizer hosted at the edge, ensuring that the latency from the streaming packager to this distribution 
optimizer remains less than 5 ms.
