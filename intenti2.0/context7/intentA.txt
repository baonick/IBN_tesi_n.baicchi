The gaming provider cloudGamingOp requires a service to manage 40,000 active gamers. This service executes video games on powerful servers 
and sends the video stream to the user, guaranteeing a very low latency to avoid making the game unplayable. The deployed service chain 
includes, in order: a game executor allocated in the cloud, a stream packer allocated in the cloud, and a delivery optimizer allocated 
at the edge. Regarding requirements, encoding is applied after the game executor. Furthermore, it is strictly required that the latency 
from the stream packer to the delivery optimizer remains below 30 ms. Finally, the provider prefers that the game executor and the stream 
packer operate on dedicated nodes.
