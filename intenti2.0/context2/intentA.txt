The cybersecurity provider secureMailOp requires a service to manage 10,000 corporate mailboxes. This pipeline intercepts incoming emails, 
removes phishing links, analyzes attachments in an isolated environment, and delivers only safe messages to the server. The service chain includes, 
in order: a mail gateway allocated at the edge, a phishing filter located in the cloud, a sandbox scanner located in the cloud, and a safe delivery 
node located in the cloud. Regarding requirements, decoding must be applied after the mail gateway. Furthermore, the provider prefers to keep the 
latency from the mail gateway to the phishing filter below 50 milliseconds. It is strictly required that the bandwidth from the phishing filter to 
the sandbox scanner is higher than 100 Mbps. Finally, logging is mandatory before the sandbox scanner.
