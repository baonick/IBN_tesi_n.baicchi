The digital provider govDataOp requires an open data portal service to manage 30,000 dataset downloads per day. This system extracts datasets from public databases, 
normalizes them into open formats, adds search metadata, and makes them available for public download. Strictly requiring that the response latency from the initial 
gathering tool to the distribution endpoint remains below 200 ms, the service begins with a data extractor allocated at the edge. Specifying that data caching is 
applied after the transformation engine, the chain connects to a format normalizer located in the cloud. Mandating that this transformation engine and the 
subsequent metadata enricher are hosted together on the exact same physical server, and preferring that the network bandwidth between them remains higher 
than 1000 mbps, the chain incorporates both blocks. Finally, the system completes the pipeline with a download provider located in the cloud.
