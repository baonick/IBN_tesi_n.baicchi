The media provider mediaBroadcastOp requires a live multicamera streaming service to support 30,000 concurrent viewers. 
This system receives video streams from different angles, allows cloud mixing, adds advertising graphics, and distributes the signal to 
thousands of viewers simultaneously. The deployed service chain includes, in order: a stream ingester allocated at the edge, a cloud studio 
located in the cloud, a graphic injector located in the cloud, and a stream distributor located in the cloud. Regarding requirements, 
compression is applied after the stream ingester. Furthermore, it is strictly required that the bandwidth from the graphic injector to 
the stream distributor remains higher than 5000 mbps. Additionally, the provider prefers that the total chain hardware is lower than 150 gb. 
Finally, caching is executed before the stream distributor.
