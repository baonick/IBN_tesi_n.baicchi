The media provider mediaBroadcastOp requires a live multicamera streaming service to support 30,000 concurrent viewers. 
This system receives video streams from different angles, allows cloud mixing, adds advertising graphics, and distributes the signal to 
thousands of viewers simultaneously. The service chain begins with a stream ingester allocated at the edge. For this component, 
compression is applied after it. The chain then connects to a cloud studio located in the cloud followed by a graphic injector located in the 
cloud. Between this graphic injector and the next component, it is strictly required to maintain a bandwidth higher than 5000 mbps. Finally, 
the traffic is routed to a stream distributor located in the cloud, ensuring that caching is executed before it. For the entire deployment, 
it is ideally preferred that the total chain hardware remains lower than 150 gb.
