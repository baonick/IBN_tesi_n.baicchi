The media provider mediaBroadcastOp requires a live multicamera streaming service to support 30,000 concurrent viewers. 
This system receives video streams from different angles, allows cloud mixing, adds advertising graphics, and distributes the signal to 
thousands of viewers simultaneously. Specifying that compression is applied after the initial ingesting function, the service begins with a 
stream ingester allocated at the edge connected to a cloud studio located in the cloud. Mandating a bandwidth higher than 5000 mbps between 
the upcoming graphics layer and the final destination, the chain incorporates a graphic injector located in the cloud. 
Preferring that the total chain hardware is lower than 150 gb, and ensuring that caching is executed before the final distribution engine, 
the system completes the pipeline with a stream distributor located in the cloud.
