The logistics provider aeroDeliveryOp requires a drone fleet control service to manage 5,000 active delivery drones. This system manages digital flight plans, 
avoids software collisions, and ensures that the command signal is always a priority. The service chain begins with a flight telemetry ingester allocated at the edge. 
For this component, caching is applied after it. The chain then connects to a collision avoider located in the cloud, ensuring that it is strictly required for the 
latency from the flight telemetry ingester to this collision avoider to remain below 10 ms. The chain proceeds to a command prioritizer located in the cloud. 
Finally, the system routes to a route dispatcher allocated at the edge; it is ideally preferred that the bandwidth from the collision avoider to this route 
dispatcher remains higher than 2000 mbps. For the entire deployment, it is ideally preferred that the total chain hardware remains lower than 200 gb.
