The logistics provider aeroDeliveryOp requires a drone fleet control service to manage 5,000 active delivery drones. This system manages digital flight plans, 
avoids software collisions, and ensures that the command signal is always a priority. Specifying that caching is applied after the initial telemetry receiver, 
the service begins with a flight telemetry ingester allocated at the edge. Mandating that the latency from this first component to the safety engine remains 
below 10 ms, the chain connects to a collision avoider located in the cloud. Preferring that the total chain hardware remains lower than 200 gb, the chain 
incorporates a command prioritizer located in the cloud. Finally, preferring that the bandwidth from the safety engine to the final dispatch point remains 
higher than 2000 mbps, the system completes the pipeline with a route dispatcher allocated at the edge.
