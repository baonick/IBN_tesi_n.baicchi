The logistics provider aeroDeliveryOp requires a drone fleet control service to manage 5,000 active delivery drones. This system manages digital 
flight plans, avoids software collisions, and ensures that the command signal is always a priority. The deployed service chain includes, in order: 
a flight telemetry ingester allocated at the edge, a collision avoider located in the cloud, a command prioritizer located in the cloud, and a route 
dispatcher allocated at the edge. Regarding requirements, caching is applied after the flight telemetry ingester. Furthermore, the provider prefers 
that the total chain hardware remains lower than 200 gb. It is strictly required that the latency from the flight telemetry ingester to the collision 
avoider remains below 10 ms. Finally, the provider prefers that the bandwidth from the collision avoider to the route dispatcher remains higher than 2000 mbps.
