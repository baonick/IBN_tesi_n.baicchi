The compliance provider gdprComplianceOp requires a GDPR verification service to manage 5,000 corporate database connections. This service scans 
corporate databases to identify unprotected personal data, applies automatic anonymization, and generates reports for regulators. The deployed service 
chain includes, in order: a database scanner allocated at the edge, an anonymizer located in the cloud, and a report generator located in the cloud. 
Regarding requirements, privacy must be applied after the database scanner. Furthermore, it is desired that the latency from the database scanner to the 
anonymizer remains lower than 20 ms. It is strictly required that the bandwidth from the anonymizer to the report generator is higher than 50 Mbps. Finally, 
security is mandatory before the report generator.
