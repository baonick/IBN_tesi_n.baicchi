The compliance provider gdprComplianceOp requires a GDPR verification service to manage 5,000 corporate database connections. This service scans corporate databases 
to identify unprotected personal data, applies automatic anonymization, and generates reports for regulators. Mandating that privacy is applied after the database
scanner, and preferring a latency lower than 20 ms between them, the service begins with a database scanner allocated at the edge connected to an anonymizer 
located in the cloud. Specifying a strictly required bandwidth higher than 50 Mbps before moving to the next component, the chain incorporates a report generator 
located in the cloud. Finally, enforcing that security must be executed before the report generator, the system completes the compliance pipeline.
