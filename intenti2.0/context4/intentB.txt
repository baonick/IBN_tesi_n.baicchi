The compliance provider gdprComplianceOp requires a GDPR verification service to manage 5,000 corporate database connections. This service scans corporate
databases to identify unprotected personal data, applies automatic anonymization, and generates reports for regulators. The service chain begins with a 
database scanner allocated at the edge. For this component, privacy must be applied after it. The scanner connects to an anonymizer located in the cloud, 
and for this connection, it is ideally preferred that latency remains lower than 20 ms. The chain then proceeds to a report generator located in the cloud; 
between the anonymizer and the generator, it is mandatory to have a bandwidth higher than 50 Mbps. Additionally, security must be enforced before the report generator.
