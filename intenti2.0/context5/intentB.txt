The network security provider secureCryptoOp requires an encrypted traffic analysis service to support 25,000 corporate users. This service inspects HTTPS flows 
without fully decrypting them, using AI to identify malware or botnet patterns. The service chain begins with a traffic splitter allocated at the edge. For this 
component, decoding is applied after it. It is mandatory that this traffic splitter and the next feature extractor, located in the cloud, are deployed on the same 
hosting node. The chain then proceeds to an ai classifier located in the cloud, followed by an alert manager located in the cloud. For the entire deployment, it is 
ideally preferred that the total chain hardware remains lower than 80 gigabytes. Finally, the traffic is routed to a policy enforcer located in the cloud, ensuring 
that security is executed before the policy enforcer.
