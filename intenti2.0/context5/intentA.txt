The network security provider secureCryptoOp requires an encrypted traffic analysis service to support 25,000 corporate users. This service inspects HTTPS flows 
without fully decrypting them, using AI to identify malware or botnet patterns. The deployed service chain includes, in order: a traffic splitter allocated at 
the edge, a feature extractor located in the cloud, an ai classifier located in the cloud, an alert manager located in the cloud, and a policy enforcer located 
in the cloud. Regarding requirements, decoding is applied after the traffic splitter. Furthermore, the provider prefers to keep the total chain hardware lower 
than 80 gigabytes. It is mandatory that the traffic splitter and the feature extractor are deployed on the same hosting node. Finally, security is enforced 
before the policy enforcer.
