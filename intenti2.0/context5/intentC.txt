The network security provider secureCryptoOp requires an encrypted traffic analysis service to support 25,000 corporate users. This service inspects HTTPS flows 
without fully decrypting them, using AI to identify malware or botnet patterns. Specifying that decoding is applied after the first VNF, and mandating that 
this initial component and the subsequent extraction service are deployed on the same hosting node, the service begins with a traffic splitter allocated at 
the edge connected to a feature extractor located in the cloud. Preferring that the total chain hardware is lower than 80 gigabytes, the chain incorporates 
an ai classifier located in the cloud followed by an alert manager located in the cloud. Finally, ensuring that security is executed before the final enforcement point, 
the system completes the pipeline with a policy enforcer located in the cloud.
