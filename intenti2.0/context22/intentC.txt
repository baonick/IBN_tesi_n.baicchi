The healthcare provider healthCareOp requires a post-operative tele-monitoring service to manage 5,000 active patients. 
This service receives digitalized patient vital data from home, analyzes cardiac anomalies, and notifies the doctor on duty immediately in case of danger. 
Specifying that privacy is applied after the initial receiving unit, the service begins with a patient data receiver allocated at the edge connected to a 
cardiac anomaly analyzer located in the cloud. Ensuring that logging is executed before the auditing layer, the chain incorporates a medical alert logger 
located in the cloud. Finally, strictly requiring that the latency from the initial receiving unit to the final notification engine remains below 100 ms, 
and preferring that the total chain hardware remains lower than 80 gb, the system completes the pipeline with a doctor notifier located in the cloud.
