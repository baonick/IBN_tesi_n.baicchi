The healthcare provider healthCareOp requires a post-operative tele-monitoring service to manage 5,000 active patients. This service receives digitalized patient vital 
data from home, analyzes cardiac anomalies, and notifies the doctor on duty immediately in case of danger. The deployed service chain includes, in order: a patient 
data receiver allocated at the edge, a cardiac anomaly analyzer located in the cloud, a medical alert logger located in the cloud, and a doctor notifier located 
in the cloud. Regarding requirements, privacy is applied after the patient data receiver. Furthermore, logging is executed before the medical alert logger. 
It is strictly required that the latency from the patient data receiver to the doctor notifier remains below 100 ms. Finally, the provider prefers that the 
total chain hardware remains lower than 80 gb.
