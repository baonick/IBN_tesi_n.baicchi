The municipal provider smartParkOp requires a parking management backend to handle 100,000 active parking spots. This system receives occupancy alerts, 
updates the city map, and processes automatic payments. The service chain begins with a sensor data collector at the edge. For this component, compression 
is applied after it. The chain then connects to a map updater in the cloud and a payment processor in the cloud; it is mandatory that these two VNF are deployed 
with affinity dedicated to specific nodes. Encoding is executed before the payment processor. The chain then connects to a status logger in the cloud, ensuring 
that logging is executed before it. Finally, the chain includes a security enforcer in the cloud, with security applied after the payment processor. 
For the entire segment, it is strictly required that the latency from the sensor data collector to this security enforcer remains below 30 ms.
