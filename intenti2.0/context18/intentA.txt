The municipal provider smartParkOp requires a parking management backend to handle 100,000 active parking spots. This system receives occupancy alerts, 
updates the city map, and processes automatic payments. The service chain includes, in order: a sensor data collector at the edge, a map updater in the cloud, 
a payment processor in the cloud, a status logger in the cloud, and a security enforcer in the cloud. Regarding requirements, compression is applied after the 
sensor data collector. Encoding is executed before the payment processor. Security is applied after the payment processor. Logging is executed before the status logger. 
Furthermore, it is mandatory that the map updater and the payment processor are deployed with affinity dedicated to specific nodes. Finally, it is strictly 
required that the latency from the sensor data collector to the security enforcer remains below 30 ms.
