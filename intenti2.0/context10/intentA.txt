The media provider cinemaRenderOp requires a service to manage 5,000 rendering projects. This infrastructure receives raw digital scenes, 
distributes the rendering computation across hundreds of virtual nodes, and reassembles the finished frames for post-production. 
The deployed service chain includes, in order: a scene receiver allocated at the edge, an asset validator located in the cloud, 
a render distributor located in the cloud, a frame assembler located in the cloud, and a color grader located in the cloud. 
Regarding requirements, compression is applied after the scene receiver. Furthermore, the provider prefers that the total chain hardware 
remains lower than 800 gb. Finally, caching is executed before the color grader.
