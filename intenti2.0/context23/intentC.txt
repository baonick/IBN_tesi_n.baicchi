The healthcare provider medDiagnosticOp requires a radiology AI diagnostics service to manage 10,000 daily scans. 
This pipeline receives high-resolution medical images, processes them with computer vision algorithms to highlight suspicious lesions, and stores them securely. 
Specifying that data compression is applied after the initial receiving function, the service begins with a dicom ingester allocated at the edge. 
Preferring that the network bandwidth from this first component to the vision engine remains higher than 500 mbps, the chain connects to a lesion 
analyzer located in the cloud. Mandating that this vision engine and the subsequent metadata extractor are strictly deployed on dedicated infrastructure servers, 
the chain incorporates both blocks. Finally, ensuring that data encoding is executed before the final long-term storage unit, the system completes the pipeline 
with a secure archiver located in the cloud.
