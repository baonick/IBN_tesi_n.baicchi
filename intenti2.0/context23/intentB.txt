The healthcare provider medDiagnosticOp requires a radiology AI diagnostics service to manage 10,000 daily scans. 
This pipeline receives high-resolution medical images, processes them with computer vision algorithms to highlight suspicious lesions, and stores them securely. 
The service chain begins with a dicom ingester allocated at the edge, specifying that data compression is applied after it. The chain then connects to a lesion 
analyzer located in the cloud and a metadata extractor located in the cloud; for these two components, it is mandatory that they are isolated on dedicated hardware 
nodes, while ensuring the bandwidth from the initial ingester to the lesion analyzer remains higher than 500 mbps. Finally, the system routes the data to a secure 
archiver located in the cloud, ensuring that data encoding is executed before it.
