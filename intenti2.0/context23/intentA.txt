The healthcare provider medDiagnosticOp requires a radiology AI diagnostics service to manage 10,000 daily scans. This pipeline receives high-resolution 
medical images, processes them with computer vision algorithms to highlight suspicious lesions, and stores them securely. The deployed service chain includes, 
in order: a dicom ingester allocated at the edge, a lesion analyzer located in the cloud, a metadata extractor located in the cloud, and a secure archiver 
located in the cloud. Regarding operational requirements, data compression is applied after the dicom ingester, while ensuring that the network bandwidth 
from this initial ingestion point to the lesion analyzer remains higher than 500 mbps. To guarantee maximum computing performance, it is mandatory that the 
lesion analyzer and the metadata extractor are hosted on completely separate, dedicated physical servers. Finally, data encoding is executed before the secure 
archiver for permanent storage.
