The media provider vodStreamOp requires a video on demand distribution service to manage 80,000 active subscribers. 
This service optimizes movie delivery by encrypting content with DRM, compressing it into different formats, and storing it on 
nodes close to the user to avoid buffering. The deployed service chain includes, in order: a DRM encryptor allocated in the cloud, 
a transcoder allocated in the cloud, and a caching node allocated at the edge. Regarding requirements, encoding is applied after 
the DRM encryptor. Furthermore, it is strictly required that the bandwidth from the transcoder to the caching 
node remains higher than 10000 mbps. Finally, the provider prefers that the DRM encryptor and the transcoder operate on the same node, 
while decoding is applied before the caching node.
