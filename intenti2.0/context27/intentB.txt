The marketing provider sentimentOp requires a customer sentiment analysis platform to manage 100,000 daily reviews and comments. This software scans social media 
inputs, identifies prevailing emotions toward specific products, and sends weekly reports to the marketing department. The service chain begins with a social data 
receiver allocated at the edge, specifying that data privacy mechanisms are applied after it. The chain then connects to a sentiment analyzer located in the cloud, 
ensuring that system security is executed before this component. Finally, the pipeline routes to a report dispatcher located in the cloud, mandating that this 
reporting block and the previous sentiment analyzer are hosted together on the same physical infrastructure.
