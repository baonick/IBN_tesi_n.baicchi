The marketing provider sentimentOp requires a customer sentiment analysis platform to manage 100,000 daily reviews and comments. This software scans social media 
inputs, identifies prevailing emotions toward specific products, and sends weekly reports to the marketing department. The deployed service chain includes, 
in order: a social data receiver allocated at the edge, a sentiment analyzer located in the cloud, and a report dispatcher located in the cloud. Regarding 
system constraints, data privacy mechanisms are applied after the social data receiver, while system security is executed before the sentiment analyzer to 
safeguard the AI processing logic. Finally, to eliminate data transfer overhead, it is mandatory that the sentiment analyzer and the report dispatcher are 
co-located on the same physical hosting node.
