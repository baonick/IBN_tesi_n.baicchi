The digital provider secureVoteOp requires a certified electronic voting service to manage 200,000 voters. This platform for public or corporate consultations 
guarantees vote anonymity and prevents data manipulation. Preferring that the network bandwidth from the entry unit to the next phase remains higher than 
100 mbps, the service begins with a ballot ingester allocated at the edge connected to an anonymizer located in the cloud. Specifying that user privacy is 
applied after this anonymization process, and ensuring that security is executed before the verification phase, the chain incorporates an integrity enforcer 
located in the cloud. The pipeline proceeds to a vote counter located in the cloud. Finally, ensuring that logging is executed before the long-term auditing 
unit, the system completes the pipeline with an audit logger located in the cloud.
