The digital provider secureVoteOp requires a certified electronic voting service to manage 200,000 voters. This platform for public or corporate consultations 
guarantees vote anonymity and prevents data manipulation. The deployed service chain includes, in order: a ballot ingester allocated at the edge, an anonymizer 
located in the cloud, an integrity enforcer located in the cloud, a vote counter located in the cloud, and an audit logger located in the cloud. 
Regarding requirements, the provider prefers that the network bandwidth from the ballot ingester to the anonymizer remains higher than 100 mbps. 
Furthermore, user privacy is applied after the anonymizer. Security is executed before the integrity enforcer to protect the system from external manipulation. 
Finally, logging is executed before the audit logger to compile compliance history.
