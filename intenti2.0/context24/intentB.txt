The digital provider secureVoteOp requires a certified electronic voting service to manage 200,000 voters. This platform for public or corporate consultations 
guarantees vote anonymity and prevents data manipulation. The service chain begins with a ballot ingester allocated at the edge. The chain then connects 
to an anonymizer located in the cloud, ensuring that the network bandwidth from the initial ingester to this anonymization component remains higher than 
100 mbps, while user privacy is applied after it. The pipeline continues to an integrity enforcer located in the cloud, ensuring that security is executed before it. 
The system then routes the data to a vote counter located in the cloud. Finally, the processed data flows to an audit logger located in the cloud, ensuring 
that logging is executed before it.
