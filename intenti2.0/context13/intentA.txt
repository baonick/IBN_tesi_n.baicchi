The financial provider fraudSafeOp requires an antifraud analysis service to manage 200,000 transactions per hour. This system intercepts every digital 
payment request, compares it with habitual user spending patterns via machine learning, and authorizes the transaction in a few milliseconds. 
The deployed service chain includes, in order: a request interceptor at the edge, a profile matcher at the edge, an ml analyzer in the cloud, a policy enforcer 
in the cloud, and a response emitter in the cloud. Regarding requirements, security is applied after the request interceptor. Furthermore, it is strictly required 
that the request interceptor and the profile matcher are deployed on the same hosting node. Privacy is executed before the ml analyzer. Finally, the provider prefers 
that the bandwidth from the ml analyzer to the policy enforcer remains higher than 1000 mbps, and that the total chain hardware remains lower than 120 gb.
