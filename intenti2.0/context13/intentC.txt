The financial provider fraudSafeOp requires an antifraud analysis service to manage 200,000 transactions per hour. This system intercepts every digital 
payment request, compares it with habitual user spending patterns via machine learning, and authorizes the transaction in a few milliseconds. 
Specifying that security is applied after the initial interception phase, and mandating that the request interceptor and the profile matcher are deployed 
on the same edge node, the service begins with a request interceptor allocated at the edge connected to a profile matcher at the edge. Ensuring that privacy 
is executed before the categorization phase, the chain incorporates an ml analyzer located in the cloud. Preferring that the bandwidth from the ml analyzer to 
the policy enforcer remains higher than 1000 mbps, the chain adds a policy enforcer located in the cloud. Finally, preferring that the total chain hardware 
remains lower than 120 gb, the system completes the pipeline with a response emitter located in the cloud.
