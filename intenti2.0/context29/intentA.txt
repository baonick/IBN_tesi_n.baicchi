The digital provider subscriptionOp requires a SaaS subscription management service to manage 50,000 active accounts. This recurring billing service handles 
customer payment cycles, software plan upgrades, and automated reminders for expired credit cards. The deployed service chain includes, in order: 
a subscription ingester allocated at the edge, a billing cycle processor located in the cloud, a dunning manager located in the cloud, and an invoice 
generator located in the cloud. Regarding operational requirements, customer privacy is applied after the subscription ingester. To interpret the incoming 
API payloads safely, decoding is executed before the billing cycle processor. Furthermore, transaction logging is executed before the dunning manager to 
keep historical track of payment failures. Finally, data encoding is executed before the invoice generator to protect sensitive financial records.
