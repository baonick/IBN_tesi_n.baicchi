The transport company needs to coordinate a fleet of 500 autonomous buses. The chain is composed, in order, of a vehicular receiver located at the edge, a path coordinator at the edge, and a fleet registry in the cloud. Moving to the specifications, a security function must process the input data to the path coordinator. It is mandatory that the total hardware resource usage of the chain is less than 64 gigabytes. It is preferred that the overall availability of the chain is higher than 99.99%.
