--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The logistics provider aeroDeliveryOp requires a drone fleet control service to manage 5,000 active delivery drones. This system manages digital flight plans, 
avoids software collisions, and ensures that the command signal is always a priority. The service chain begins with a <flightTelemetryIngesterVF, 12> ingester allocated at the edge. 
For this component, caching is applied after it. The chain then connects to a <collisionAvoiderVF, 32> located in the cloud, ensuring that it is strictly required for the 
latency from the <flightTelemetryIngesterVF, 12> ingester to this <collisionAvoiderVF, 32> to remain below 10 ms. The chain proceeds to a <commandPrioritizerVF, 16> located in the cloud. 
Finally, the system routes to a <routeDispatcherVF, 12> allocated at the edge; it is ideally preferred that the bandwidth from the <collisionAvoiderVF, 32> to this <routeDispatcherVF, 12> remains higher than 2000 mbps. For the entire deployment, it is ideally preferred that the total chain hardware remains lower than 200 gb.
