--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The media provider vodStreamOp requires a video on demand distribution service to manage 80,000 active subscribers. 
This service optimizes movie delivery by encrypting content with DRM, compressing it into different formats, and storing it on nodes 
close to the user to avoid buffering. The service chain begins with a <drmEncryptorVF, 16> allocated in the cloud. For this component, encoding 
is applied after it. It is ideally preferred that this <drmEncryptorVF, 16> and the next component, a <transcoderVF, 32> allocated in the cloud, 
are deployed on the same hosting node. The chain then proceeds to a <cachingNodeVF, 12> node allocated at the edge; between the <transcoderVF, 32> and 
this <cachingNodeVF, 12> node, it is strictly required to maintain a bandwidth higher than 10000 mbps. Additionally, decoding must be executed 
before the <cachingNodeVF, 12> node.
