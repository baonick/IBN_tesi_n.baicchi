--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The financial provider fraudSafeOp requires an antifraud analysis service to manage 200,000 transactions per hour. This system intercepts every digital payment 
request, compares it with habitual user spending patterns via machine learning, and authorizes the transaction in a few milliseconds. 
The service chain begins with a <requestInterceptorVF, 8> allocated at the edge. For this component, security is applied after it. It is mandatory that this <requestInterceptorVF, 8> and the next component, a <profileMatcherVF, 16>, operate on the same node at the edge. The chain then connects to an <mlAnalyzerVF, 32> located in the cloud, 
ensuring that privacy is executed before it. The chain then proceeds to a <policyEnforcerVF, 12> located in the cloud; it is ideally preferred that the bandwidth from 
the <mlAnalyzerVF, 32> to this <policyEnforcerVF, 12> remains higher than 1000 mbps. Finally, the system routes to a <responseEmitterVF, 8> located in the cloud, with a preference 
that the total chain hardware remains lower than 120 gb.
