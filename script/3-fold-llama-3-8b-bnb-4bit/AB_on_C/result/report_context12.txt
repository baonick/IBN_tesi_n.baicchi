--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The enterprise provider corpDocOp requires a document classification engine to manage 50,000 daily files. This system analyzes PDF 
files or OCR scans, identifies the document type like invoice, contract, or reminder, and routes it to the competent business function. 
Specifying that security is applied after the initial phase, and mandating that this first component and the <textExtractorVF, 12> are deployed on the same hosting node, the service begins with an <ocrScannerVF, 16> allocated at the edge connected to a <textExtractorVF, 12> 
located in the edge. Strictly requiring that the latency from the <textExtractorVF, 12> to the <aiDocumentClassifierVF, 32> remains below 50 ms, the 
chain incorporates an <aiDocumentClassifierVF, 32> located in the cloud. Ensuring that privacy is executed before the <dataRedactorVF, 16>, the chain connects 
to a <dataRedactorVF, 16> located in the cloud. Finally, preferring a bandwidth higher than 500 mbps between the <aiDocumentClassifierVF, 32> and the final 
distribution point, the system completes the pipeline with a <documentRouterVF, 8> located in the cloud.
