--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The cybersecurity provider secureMailOp requires a service to manage 10,000 corporate mailboxes. This pipeline intercepts incoming emails, 
removes phishing links, analyzes attachments in an isolated environment, and delivers only safe messages to the server. The service chain begins 
with a <mailGatewayVF, 4> allocated at the edge. For this node, it is strictly required that decoding is applied after it. This <mailGatewayVF, 4> connects to a 
<phishingFilterVF, 8> located in the cloud, and for this link, it is ideally preferred that latency remains below 50 milliseconds. The chain then proceeds 
to a <sandboxScannerVF, 16> located in the cloud; between the filter and the scanner, it is mandatory to have a bandwidth higher than 100 Mbps. Additionally, 
logging must be enforced before the <sandboxScannerVF, 16>. Finally, the scanner connects to a <safeDeliveryVF, 4> node located in the cloud.
