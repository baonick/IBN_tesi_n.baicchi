--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The website security provider ddosProtectionOp requires an anti-DDoS defense system to handle 50,000 active web users. 
This system analyzes incoming traffic, cleans malicious flows, and ensures server availability. The service chain starts with a <networkTrafficAnalyzerVF, 8> located at the edge. 
A bandwidth higher than 500 megabps is strictly required from this <networkTrafficAnalyzerVF, 8> to a <scrubbingFilterVF, 32> located in the cloud. For this <scrubbingFilterVF, 32>, it is mandatory 
that security is executed before it. The chain then connects to a <rateLimiterVF, 16> located in the cloud. It is ideally preferred that logging is applied after 
the <rateLimiterVF, 16>. Finally, the traffic is directed to a <webServerVF, 12> located in the cloud, where a latency lower than 10 ms is desired between the <rateLimiterVF, 16> 
and the <webServerVF, 12>.
