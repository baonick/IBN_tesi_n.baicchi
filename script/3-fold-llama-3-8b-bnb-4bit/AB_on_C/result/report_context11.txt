--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The media provider liveSubsOp requires a live subtitling service to manage 10,000 concurrent streams. This service intercepts the audio 
of a streaming event, transforms it into text via AI, translates it into three languages, and reinserts it into the video feed as subtitles. 
Specifying that caching is applied after the initial function, the service begins with an <audioExtractorVF, 8> allocated at the edge 
connected to a <speechToTextVF, 32> located in the cloud and a <multiTranslatorVF, 24> located in the cloud. Preferring that the total chain 
hardware is lower than 100 gb, and strictly requiring that the latency from the <speechToTextVF, 32> to the final node remains 
below 20 ms, the system completes the pipeline with a <subtitleInjectorVF, 12> allocated at the edge.
