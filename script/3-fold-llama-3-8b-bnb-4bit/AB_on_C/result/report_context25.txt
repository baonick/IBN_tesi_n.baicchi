normalizes them into open formats, adds search metadata, and makes them available for public download. Strictly requiring that the response latency from the initial 
function to the final VNF below 200 ms, the service begins with a data extractor allocated at the edge. Specifying caching is 
applied after the <formatNormalizerVF, 16>, the chain connects to a <formatNormalizerVF, 16> located in the cloud. Mandating that this <formatNormalizerVF, 16> and the 
subsequent <metadataEnricherVF, 12> are hosted together on the exact same physical server, and preferring that the network bandwidth between them remains higher 
than 1000 mbps, the chain incorporates both blocks in cloud. Finally, the system completes the pipeline with a <downloadProviderVF, 16> located in the cloud.
