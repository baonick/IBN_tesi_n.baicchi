--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The service provider eInvoiceOp requires a digital invoicing service to manage 50,000 invoices per day. This system transforms sales data into certified XML files, 
applies the digital signature, and automatically sends them to governmental systems for fiscal validation. Mandating that the total chain hardware remains lower 
than 150 gb, the service begins with a <saleDataCollectorVF, 8> allocated at the edge connected to an <xmlConverterVF, 16> located in the cloud. Strictly requiring that the 
latency from the <xmlConverterVF, 16> to the <digitalSignerVF, 12> remains below 30 ms, the chain incorporates a <digitalSignerVF, 12> located in the cloud. Finally, preferring 
that the bandwidth from the <digitalSignerVF, 12> to the final point remains higher than 100 mbps, the system completes the pipeline with a <taxAuthorityTransmitterVF, 8> located in the cloud.
