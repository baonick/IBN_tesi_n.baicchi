--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The IT provider corpITOp requires a service to manage 5,000 office employees. This service must filter dangerous websites, 
scan downloaded files for malware, and ensure that sensitive data does not leave the network. The service chain includes, 
in order: a <webFilterVF, 4> allocated at the edge, a <malwareScannerVF, 16> located in the cloud, and a <dlpEngineVF, 8> located in the cloud. 
Regarding requirements, it is a mandatory requirement that the latency from <webFilterVF, 4> to the <malwareScannerVF, 16> remains below 10 milliseconds. 
Furthermore, the bandwidth between the <malwareScannerVF, 16> and the <dlpEngineVF, 8> must be greater than 500 mbps.
