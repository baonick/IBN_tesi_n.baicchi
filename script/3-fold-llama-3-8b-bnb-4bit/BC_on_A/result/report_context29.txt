--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The digital provider subscriptionOp requires a SaaS subscription management service to manage 50,000 active accounts. This recurring billing service handles 
customer payment cycles, software plan upgrades, and automated reminders for expired credit cards. The deployed service chain includes, in order: 
a <subscriptionIngesterVF, 12> allocated at the edge, a <billingCycleProcessorVF, 24> located in the cloud, a <dunningManagerVF, 16> located in the cloud, and an <invoiceGeneratorVF, 16> located in the cloud. Regarding operational requirements, customer privacy is applied after the <subscriptionIngesterVF, 12>. To interpret the incoming 
API payloads safely, decoding is executed before the <billingCycleProcessorVF, 24>. Furthermore, transaction logging is executed before the <dunningManagerVF, 16> to 
keep historical track of payment failures. Finally, encoding is executed before the <invoiceGeneratorVF, 16> to protect sensitive financial records.
