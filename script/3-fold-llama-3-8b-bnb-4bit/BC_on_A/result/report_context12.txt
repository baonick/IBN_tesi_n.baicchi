--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The enterprise provider corpDocOp requires a document classification engine to manage 50,000 daily files. This system analyzes PDF 
files or OCR scans, identifies the document type like invoice, contract, or reminder, and routes it to the competent business function. 
The service chain begins with an ocrScannerVF allocated at the edge. For this component, security is applied after it. It is mandatory that 
this ocrScannerVF and the next textExtractorVF, located in the edge, are deployed on the same hosting node. The chain then connects to an aiDocumentClassifierVF located in the cloud; for this connection, it is strictly required that the latency from the textExtractorVF to the aiDocumentClassifierVF 
remains below 50 ms. The chain proceeds to a dataRedactorVF located in the cloud, ensuring that privacy is executed before it. Finally, the 
files are routed to a documentRouterVF located in the cloud, and it is ideally preferred that the bandwidth from the aiDocumentClassifierVF to this 
documentRouterVF remains higher than 500 mbps.
