--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The service provider eInvoiceOp requires a digital invoicing service to manage 50,000 invoices per day. This system transforms sales data into certified XML files, 
applies the digital signature, and automatically sends them to governmental systems for fiscal validation. The deployed service chain includes, in order: a <saleDataCollectorVF, 8> allocated at the edge, an <xmlConverterVF, 16> located in the cloud, a <digitalSignerVF, 12> located in the cloud, and a <taxAuthorityTransmitterVF, 8> transmitter located in the cloud. 
Regarding requirements, the provider mandates that the total chain hardware remains lower than 150 gb. Furthermore, it is strictly required that the latency from the 
<xmlConverterVF, 16> to the <digitalSignerVF, 12> remains below 30 ms. Finally, the provider prefers that the bandwidth from the <digitalSignerVF, 12> to the <taxAuthorityTransmitterVF, 8> transmitter 
remains higher than 100 mbps.
