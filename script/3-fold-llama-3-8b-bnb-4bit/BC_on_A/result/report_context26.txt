--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The educational provider eduCloudOp requires a virtual student lab platform to support 10,000 concurrent students. This system provides 
temporary programming environments, manages resource allocation for each student, and monitors the workspaces to prevent improper use. The deployed service 
chain includes, in order: a <sessionIngesterVF, 8> allocated at the edge, a <resourceAllocatorVF, 16> located in the cloud, an <activityMonitorVF, 24> located in the cloud, a <userPrivacyEnforcerVF, 12> located in the cloud, an <auditLoggerVF, 12> located in the cloud, and an <outputPackagerVF, 16> located in the cloud. Regarding constraints, data privacy 
mechanisms are applied after the <sessionIngesterVF, 8>. To detect anomalies early, security are executed before the <activityMonitorVF, 24>, while ensuring that 
the latency time between the initial <sessionIngesterVF, 8> and this <activityMonitorVF, 24> is kept under 40 ms. Furthermore, logging operations are executed 
before the <auditLoggerVF, 12>. Data compression is applied after the final <outputPackagerVF, 16>. For the network backbone, the provider requires that the bandwidth between 
the <sessionIngesterVF, 8> and the <resourceAllocatorVF, 16> supports more than 500 mbps. Finally, the provider prefers that the aggregate infrastructure footprint stays below 120 gb.
