--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The financial provider fraudSafeOp requires an antifraud analysis service to manage 200,000 transactions per hour. This system intercepts every digital 
payment request, compares it with habitual user spending patterns via machine learning, and authorizes the transaction in a few milliseconds. 
The deployed service chain includes, in order: a <requestInterceptorVF, 8> at the edge, a <profileMatcherVF, 16> at the edge, an <mlAnalyzerVF, 32> in the cloud, a <policyEnforcerVF, 12> 
in the cloud, and a <responseEmitterVF, 8> in the cloud. Regarding requirements, security is applied after the <requestInterceptorVF, 8>. Furthermore, it is strictly required 
that the <requestInterceptorVF, 8> and the <profileMatcherVF, 16> are deployed on the same hosting node. Privacy is executed before the <mlAnalyzerVF, 32>. Finally, the provider prefers 
that the bandwidth from the <mlAnalyzerVF, 32> to the <policyEnforcerVF, 12> remains higher than 1000 mbps, and that the total chain hardware remains lower than 120 gb.
