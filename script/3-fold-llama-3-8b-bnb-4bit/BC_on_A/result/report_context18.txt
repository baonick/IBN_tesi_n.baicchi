--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The municipal provider smartParkOp requires a parking management backend to handle 100,000 active parking spots. This system receives occupancy alerts, 
updates the city map, and processes automatic payments. The service chain begins with a sensorDataCollectorVF at the edge. For this component, compression 
is applied after it. The chain then connects to a mapUpdaterVF in the cloud and a paymentProcessorVF in the cloud; it is mandatory that these two VNF are deployed 
on dedicated nodes. Encoding is executed before the paymentProcessorVF. The chain then connects to a statusLoggerVF in the cloud, ensuring 
that logging is executed before it. Finally, the chain includes a securityEnforcerVF in the cloud, with security applied after the paymentProcessorVF. 
For the entire segment, it is strictly required that the latency from the sensorDataCollectorVF to this securityEnforcerVF remains below 30 ms.
