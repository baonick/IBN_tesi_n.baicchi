--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The municipal provider smartParkOp requires a parking management backend to handle 100,000 active parking spots. This system receives occupancy alerts, 
updates the city map, and processes automatic payments. The service chain begins with a <sensorDataCollectorVF, 8> at the edge. For this component, compression 
is applied after it. The chain then connects to a <mapUpdaterVF, 16> in the cloud and a <paymentProcessorVF, 24> in the cloud; it is mandatory that these two VNF are deployed 
on dedicated nodes. Encoding is executed before the <paymentProcessorVF, 24>. The chain then connects to a <statusLoggerVF, 12> in the cloud, ensuring 
that logging is executed before it. Finally, the chain includes a <securityEnforcerVF, 16> in the cloud, with security applied after the <paymentProcessorVF, 24>. 
For the entire segment, it is strictly required that the latency from the <sensorDataCollectorVF, 8> to this <securityEnforcerVF, 16> remains below 30 ms.
