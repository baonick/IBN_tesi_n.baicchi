--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The municipal provider smartParkOp requires a parking management backend to handle 100,000 active parking spots. This system receives occupancy alerts, 
updates the city map, and processes automatic payments. The service chain includes, in order: a <sensorDataCollectorVF, 8> at the edge, a <mapUpdaterVF, 16> in the cloud, 
a <paymentProcessorVF, 24> in the cloud, a <statusLoggerVF, 12> in the cloud, and a <securityEnforcerVF, 16> in the cloud. Regarding requirements, compression is applied after the 
<sensorDataCollectorVF, 8>. Encoding is executed before the <paymentProcessorVF, 24>. Security is applied after the <paymentProcessorVF, 24>. Logging is executed before the <statusLoggerVF, 12>. 
Furthermore, it is mandatory that the <mapUpdaterVF, 16> and the <paymentProcessorVF, 24> are deployed on dedicated nodes. Finally, it is strictly 
required that the latency from the <sensorDataCollectorVF, 8> to the <securityEnforcerVF, 16> remains below 30 ms.
