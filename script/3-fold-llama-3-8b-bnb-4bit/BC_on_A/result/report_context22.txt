--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The healthcare provider healthCareOp requires a post-operative tele-monitoring service to manage 5,000 active patients. This service receives digitalized patient vital 
data from home, analyzes cardiac anomalies, and notifies the doctor on duty immediately in case of danger. The deployed service chain includes, in order: a <patientDataReceiverVF, 8> allocated at the edge, a <cardiacAnomalyAnalyzerVF, 24> located in the cloud, a <medicalAlertLoggerVF, 12> located in the cloud, and a <doctorNotifierVF, 8> located 
in the cloud. Regarding requirements, privacy is applied after the <patientDataReceiverVF, 8>. Furthermore, logging is executed before the <medicalAlertLoggerVF, 12>. 
It is strictly required that the latency from the <patientDataReceiverVF, 8> to the <doctorNotifierVF, 8> remains below 100 ms. Finally, the provider prefers that the 
total chain hardware remains lower than 80 gb.
