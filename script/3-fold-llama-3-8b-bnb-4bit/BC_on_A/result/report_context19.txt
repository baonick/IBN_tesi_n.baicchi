--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The logistics provider aeroDeliveryOp requires a drone fleet control service to manage 5,000 active delivery drones. This system manages digital 
flight plans, avoids software collisions, and ensures that the command signal is always a priority. The deployed service chain includes, in order: 
a <flightTelemetryIngesterVF, 12> allocated at the edge, a <collisionAvoiderVF, 32> located in the cloud, a <commandPrioritizerVF, 16> located in the cloud, and a <routeDispatcherVF, 12> allocated at the edge. Regarding requirements, caching is applied after the <flightTelemetryIngesterVF, 12>. Furthermore, the provider prefers 
that the total chain hardware remains lower than 200 gb. It is strictly required that the latency from the <flightTelemetryIngesterVF, 12> to the <collisionAvoiderVF, 32> remains below 10 ms. Finally, the provider prefers that the bandwidth from the <collisionAvoiderVF, 32> to the <routeDispatcherVF, 12> remains higher than 2000 mbps.
