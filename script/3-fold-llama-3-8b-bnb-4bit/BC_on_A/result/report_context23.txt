--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The healthcare provider medDiagnosticOp requires a radiology AI diagnostics service to manage 10,000 daily scans. 
This pipeline receives high-resolution medical images, processes them with computer vision algorithms to highlight suspicious lesions, and stores them securely. 
The service chain begins with a dicomIngesterVF allocated at the edge, specifying compression is applied after it. The chain then connects to a lesionAnalyzerVF located in the cloud and a metadataExtractorVF located in the cloud; for these two components, it is mandatory that they are isolated on dedicated hardware 
nodes, while ensuring the bandwidth from the dicomIngesterVF to the lesionAnalyzerVF remains higher than 500 mbps. Finally, the system routes the data to a secure 
secureArchiverVF in the cloud, ensuring that data encoding is executed before it.
