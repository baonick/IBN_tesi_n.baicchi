--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The healthcare provider medDiagnosticOp requires a radiology AI diagnostics service to manage 10,000 daily scans. This pipeline receives high-resolution 
medical images, processes them with computer vision algorithms to highlight suspicious lesions, and stores them securely. The deployed service chain includes, 
in order: a <dicomIngesterVF, 16> allocated at the edge, a <lesionAnalyzerVF, 32> located in the cloud, a <metadataExtractorVF, 12> located in the cloud, and a <secureArchiverVF, 24> 
located in the cloud. Regarding operational requirements compression is applied after the <dicomIngesterVF, 16>, while ensuring that the network bandwidth 
from this initial <dicomIngesterVF, 16> to the <lesionAnalyzerVF, 32> remains higher than 500 mbps. To guarantee maximum computing performance, it is mandatory that the 
<lesionAnalyzerVF, 32> and the <metadataExtractorVF, 12> are hosted on completely separate, dedicated physical servers. Finally, data encoding is executed before the <secureArchiverVF, 24> for permanent storage.
