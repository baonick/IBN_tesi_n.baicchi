--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The gaming provider cloudGamingOp requires a service to manage 40,000 active gamers. This service executes video games on powerful servers 
and sends the video stream to the user, guaranteeing a very low latency to avoid making the game unplayable. The deployed service chain 
includes, in order: a <gameExecutorVF, 32> allocated in the cloud, a <streamPackerVF, 16> allocated in the cloud, and a <deliveryOptimizerVF, 8> allocated 
at the edge. Regarding requirements, encoding is applied after the <gameExecutorVF, 32>. Furthermore, it is strictly required that the latency 
from the <streamPackerVF, 16> to the <deliveryOptimizerVF, 8> remains below 30 ms. Finally, the provider prefers that the <gameExecutorVF, 32> and the <streamPackerVF, 16> operate on dedicated nodes.
