--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The website security provider ddosProtectionOp requires an anti-DDoS defense system to handle 50,000 active web users. 
This system analyzes incoming traffic, cleans malicious flows, and ensures server availability. The service chain starts with a networkTrafficAnalyzerVF located at the edge. 
A bandwidth higher than 500 megabps is strictly required from this networkTrafficAnalyzerVF to a scrubbingFilterVF located in the cloud. For this scrubbingFilterVF, it is mandatory 
that security is executed before it. The chain then connects to a rateLimiterVF located in the cloud. It is ideally preferred that logging is applied after 
the rateLimiterVF. Finally, the traffic is directed to a webServerVF located in the cloud, where a latency lower than 10 ms is desired between the rateLimiterVF 
and the webServerVF.
==================================================

--- Fatti Estratti Ideale ---
intent(ddosProtectionOp,50000)

propertyExpectation(latency,lower,soft,10,ms,rateLimiterVF,webServerVF)
propertyExpectation(logging,_,rateLimiterVF,_)
propertyExpectation(security,_,_,scrubbingFilterVF)
