--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The media provider vodStreamOp requires a video on demand distribution service to manage 80,000 active subscribers. 
This service optimizes movie delivery by encrypting content with DRM, compressing it into different formats, and storing it on nodes 
close to the user to avoid buffering. The service chain begins with a drmEncryptorVF allocated in the cloud. For this component, encoding 
is applied after it. It is ideally preferred that this drmEncryptorVF and the next component, a transcoderVF allocated in the cloud, 
are deployed on the same hosting node. The chain then proceeds to a cachingNodeVF node allocated at the edge; between the transcoderVF and 
this cachingNodeVF node, it is strictly required to maintain a bandwidth higher than 10000 mbps. Additionally, decoding must be executed 
before the cachingNodeVF node.
