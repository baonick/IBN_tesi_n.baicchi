--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The industrial provider autoWarehouseOp requires a remote control system for autonomous machines to manage 500 warehouse robots. 
This software receives sensor data, calculates paths to avoid collisions, and sends steering and braking commands. The deployed service chain includes, 
in order: a <sensorDataDecoderVF, 16> allocated at the edge, a <collisionPathSolverVF, 32> located in the cloud, and a <commandEncoderVF, 16> allocated at the edge. 
Regarding requirements, decoding is applied after the <sensorDataDecoderVF, 16>. Furthermore, it is strictly required that the latency from the <sensorDataDecoderVF, 16> 
to the <commandEncoderVF, 16> remains below 500 ms. Finally, encoding is executed before the <commandEncoderVF, 16>.
