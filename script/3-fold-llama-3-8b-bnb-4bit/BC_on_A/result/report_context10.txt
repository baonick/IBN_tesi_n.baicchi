--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The media provider cinemaRenderOp requires a service to manage 5,000 rendering projects. This infrastructure receives raw digital scenes, 
distributes the rendering computation across hundreds of virtual nodes, and reassembles the finished frames for post-production. 
The deployed service chain includes, in order: a <sceneReceiverVF, 16> allocated at the edge, an <assetValidatorVF, 12> located in the cloud, 
a <renderDistributorVF, 32> located in the cloud, a <frameAssemblerVF, 24> located in the cloud, and a <colorGraderVF, 20> located in the cloud. 
Regarding requirements, compression is applied after the <sceneReceiverVF, 16>. Furthermore, the provider prefers that the total chain hardware 
remains lower than 800 gb. Finally, caching is executed before the <colorGraderVF, 20>.
