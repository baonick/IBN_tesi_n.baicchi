--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The marketing provider sentimentOp requires a customer sentiment analysis platform to manage 100,000 daily reviews and comments. This software scans social media 
inputs, identifies prevailing emotions toward specific products, and sends weekly reports to the marketing department. The deployed service chain includes, 
in order: a social <socialDataReceiverVF, 16> allocated at the edge, asentimentAnalyzerVF located in the cloud, and a <reportDispatcherVF, 16> located in the cloud. Regarding 
system constraints, privacy mechanisms are applied after the social <socialDataReceiverVF, 16>, while security is executed before the <sentimentAnalyzerVF, 32> to 
safeguard the AI processing logic. Finally, to eliminate data transfer overhead, it is mandatory that thesentimentAnalyzerVF and the <reportDispatcherVF, 16> are 
co-located on the same physical hosting node.
