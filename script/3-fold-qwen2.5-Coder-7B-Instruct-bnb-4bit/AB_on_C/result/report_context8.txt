thousands of viewers simultaneously. The service chain begins with a <streamIngesterVF, 12> allocated at the edge. For this component, 
compression is applied after it. The chain then connects to a <cloudStudioVF, 32> located in the cloud followed by a <graphicInjectorVF, 16> located in the 
cloud. Between this <graphicInjectorVF, 16> and the next component, it is strictly required to maintain a bandwidth higher than 5000 mbps. Finally, 
the traffic is routed to a <streamDistributorVF, 24> located in the cloud, ensuring that caching is executed before it. For the entire deployment, 
it is ideally preferred that the total chain hardware remains lower than 150 gb.
