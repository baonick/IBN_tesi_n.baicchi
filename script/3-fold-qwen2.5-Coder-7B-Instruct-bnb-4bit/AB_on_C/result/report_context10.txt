--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The media provider cinemaRenderOp requires a service to manage 5,000 rendering projects. This infrastructure receives raw digital scenes, 
distributes the rendering computation across hundreds of virtual nodes, and reassembles the finished frames for post-production. 
The service chain begins with a <sceneReceiverVF, 16> allocated at the edge. For this component, compression is applied after it. The chain 
then connects to an <assetValidatorVF, 12> located in the cloud followed by a <renderDistributorVF, 32> located in the cloud and a <frameAssemblerVF, 24> 
located in the cloud. For the entire deployment, it is ideally preferred that the total chain hardware remains lower than 800 gb. Finally, 
the frames are routed to a <colorGraderVF, 20> located in the cloud, ensuring that caching is executed before it.
