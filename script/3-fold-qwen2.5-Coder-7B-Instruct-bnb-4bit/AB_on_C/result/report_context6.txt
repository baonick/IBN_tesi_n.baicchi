--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The government provider govMessagingOp requires a service to manage 50,000 institutional messages. This service receives digital communications, 
verifies the electronic signature of the sender, checks its integrity, and archives the message in a legal vault. Specifying that caching is 
applied after the first function, the service begins with a <msgReceiverVF, 8> allocated at the edge. Mandating that the <sigVerifierVF, 16> and the <integrityCheckerVF, 12> are deployed on the same hosting node, the chain connects to a <sigVerifierVF, 16> located in the cloud and 
an <integrityCheckerVF, 12> located in the cloud. Preferring that the total chain hardware is lower than 100 gb, and ensuring that compression is 
executed before the final <legalVaultVF, 32>, the system completes the pipeline with a <legalVaultVF, 32> located in the cloud.
