--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The financial provider fraudSafeOp requires an antifraud analysis service to manage 200,000 transactions per hour. This system intercepts every digital 
payment request, compares it with habitual user spending patterns via machine learning, and authorizes the transaction in a few milliseconds. 
Specifying that security is applied after the initial node, and mandating that the <requestInterceptorVF, 8> and the <profileMatcherVF, 16> are deployed 
on the same edge node, the service begins with a <requestInterceptorVF, 8> allocated at the edge connected to a <profileMatcherVF, 16> at the edge. Ensuring that privacy 
is executed before the <mlAnalyzerVF, 32>, the chain incorporates an <mlAnalyzerVF, 32> located in the cloud. Preferring that the bandwidth from the <mlAnalyzerVF, 32> to 
the <policyEnforcerVF, 12> remains higher than 1000 mbps, the chain adds a <policyEnforcerVF, 12> located in the cloud. Finally, preferring that the total chain hardware 
remains lower than 120 gb, the system completes the pipeline with a <responseEmitterVF, 8> located in the cloud.
