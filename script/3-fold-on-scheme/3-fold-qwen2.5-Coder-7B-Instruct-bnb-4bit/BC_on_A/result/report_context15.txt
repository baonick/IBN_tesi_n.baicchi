--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The financial provider onlineBankOp requires a digital identity verification service to manage 10,000 daily uploads. This service processes photos of 
documents uploaded by users, verifies their authenticity, and compares the data with international blacklists to prevent money laundering. 
The deployed service chain includes, in order: a <documentReceiverVF, 12> allocated at the edge, an <authenticityCheckerVF, 24> located in the cloud, an <amlMatcherVF, 16> 
located in the cloud, and a <profileCreatorVF, 16> located in the cloud. Regarding requirements, encoding is applied after the <documentReceiverVF, 12>. Security is 
executed before the <authenticityCheckerVF, 24>. Privacy is applied after the <amlMatcherVF, 16>. Finally, compression is executed before the <profileCreatorVF, 16>.
