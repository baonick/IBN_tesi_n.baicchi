--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The website security provider ddosProtectionOp requires an anti-DDoS defense system to handle 50,000 active web users. This system analyzes incoming traffic, 
cleans malicious flows, and ensures server availability. The deployed service chain consists of: a <networkTrafficAnalyzerVF, 8> located at the edge, a <scrubbingFilterVF, 32> 
located in the cloud, a <rateLimiterVF, 16> located in the cloud, and a <webServerVF, 12> located in the cloud. For security operations, it is mandatory that security is 
applied before the <scrubbingFilterVF, 32>. Furthermore, it is strictly required that the bandwidth between the <networkTrafficAnalyzerVF, 8> and the <scrubbingFilterVF, 32> is greater 
than 500 megabps. Additionally, the provider prefers to enforce logging after the <rateLimiterVF, 16>. Finally, it is desired that the latency from the <rateLimiterVF, 16> 
to the <webServerVF, 12> remains lower than 10 ms.
