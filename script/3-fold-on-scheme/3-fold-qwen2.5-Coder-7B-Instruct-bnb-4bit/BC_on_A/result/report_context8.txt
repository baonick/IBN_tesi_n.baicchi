thousands of viewers simultaneously. The deployed service chain includes, in order: a <streamIngesterVF, 12> allocated at the edge, a <cloudStudioVF, 32> 
located in the cloud, a <graphicInjectorVF, 16> located in the cloud, and a <streamDistributorVF, 24> located in the cloud. Regarding requirements, 
compression is applied after the <streamIngesterVF, 12>. Furthermore, it is strictly required that the bandwidth from the <graphicInjectorVF, 16> to 
the <streamDistributorVF, 24> remains higher than 5000 mbps. Additionally, the provider prefers that the total chain hardware is lower than 150 gb. 
Finally, caching is executed before the <streamDistributorVF, 24>.
