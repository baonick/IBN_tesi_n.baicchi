--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The software provider devToolsOp requires a code efficiency analyzer service to manage 20,000 daily evaluation requests. This platform receives code snippets, 
executes a simulated runtime execution, and suggests specific optimizations to reduce computational resource consumption. 
The deployed service chain includes, in order: a <codeReceiverVF, 8> allocated at the edge, a <syntaxValidatorVF, 12> located in the cloud, 
a <sandboxSimulatorVF, 32> located in the cloud, an <optimizationEngineVF, 24> located in the cloud, and a <developersReportGeneratorVF, 16> located in the cloud. 
Regarding operational requirements, caching is applied after the <syntaxValidatorVF, 12> to avoid processing identical code inputs. Furthermore, 
data compression is applied after the final <developersReportGeneratorVF, 16> to streamline delivery. Finally, the provider prefers that the total chain hardware 
remains lower than 110 gb to minimize overall infrastructure overhead.
