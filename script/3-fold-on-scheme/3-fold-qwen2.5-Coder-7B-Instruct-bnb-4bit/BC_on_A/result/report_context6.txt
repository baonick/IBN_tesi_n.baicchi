--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The government provider govMessagingOp requires a service to manage 50,000 institutional messages. This service receives digital communications,
verifies the electronic signature of the sender, checks its integrity, and archives the message in a legal vault. The deployed service chain 
includes, in order: a <msgReceiverVF, 8> allocated at the edge, a <sigVerifierVF, 16> located in the cloud, an <integrityCheckerVF, 12> located in the 
cloud, and a <legalVaultVF, 32> located in the cloud. Regarding requirements, caching is applied after the <msgReceiverVF, 8>. Furthermore, the provider 
prefers to keep the total chain hardware lower than 100 gb. It is a mandatory requirement that the <sigVerifierVF, 16> and the <integrityCheckerVF, 12> 
operate on the same node. Finally, compression is applied before the <legalVaultVF, 32>.
