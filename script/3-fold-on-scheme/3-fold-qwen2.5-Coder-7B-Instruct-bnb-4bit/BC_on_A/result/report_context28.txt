--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The industrial provider factoryCloudOp requires an augmented reality back-end service to support 500 active smart glasses on the shop floor. This service 
sends visual technical instructions to workers' headsets, overlaying maintenance data on the framed machinery in real time. The deployed service chain 
includes, in order: an <arStreamReceiverVF, 12> allocated at the edge, an <arPoseEstimatorVF, 24> located in the cloud, a <dataOverlayEngineVF, 32> located in the cloud, 
and an <arStreamDispatcherVF, 16> located in the cloud. Regarding system performance, the provider requires that the network bandwidth from the initial node to the <arPoseEstimatorVF, 24> remains higher than 200 mbps. To prevent any lag in the workers' field of view, it is strictly required that the end-to-end 
latency from the <arStreamReceiverVF, 12> to the final <arStreamDispatcherVF, 16> remains below 12 ms. To avoid computational interference, it is mandatory that <arPoseEstimatorVF, 24> and the <dataOverlayEngineVF, 32> are hosted on separate, completely dedicated physical servers. Finally, the provider prefers that the total chain 
hardware remains lower than 90 gb to control operational costs.
