--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The healthcare provider healthCareOp requires a post-operative tele-monitoring service to manage 5,000 active patients. This service receives digitalized patient 
vital data from home, analyzes cardiac anomalies, and notifies the doctor on duty immediately in case of danger. The service chain begins with a <patientDataReceiverVF, 8> allocated at the edge. For this component, privacy is applied after it. The chain then connects to a <cardiacAnomalyAnalyzerVF, 24> located in the cloud. 
The next component is a <medicalAlertLoggerVF, 12> located in the cloud, ensuring that logging is executed before it. Finally, the system routes to a <doctorNotifierVF, 8> 
located in the cloud, ensuring it is strictly required that the latency from the <patientDataReceiverVF, 8> to this <doctorNotifierVF, 8> remains below 100 ms. 
For the entire deployment, it is ideally preferred that the total chain hardware remains lower than 80 gb.
