--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The media provider liveSubsOp requires a live subtitling service to manage 10,000 concurrent streams. This service intercepts the audio of a 
streaming event, transforms it into text via AI, translates it into three languages, and reinserts it into the video feed as subtitles. 
The service chain begins with an <audioExtractorVF, 8> allocated at the edge. For this component, caching is applied after it. The chain then 
connects to a <speechToTextVF, 32> located in the cloud followed by a <multiTranslatorVF, 24> located in the cloud. For the entire deployment, 
it is ideally preferred that the total chain hardware remains lower than 100 gb. Finally, the text is routed to a <subtitleInjectorVF, 12> allocated 
at the edge, ensuring that the latency from the <speechToTextVF, 32> to this <subtitleInjectorVF, 12> must remain below 20 ms.
