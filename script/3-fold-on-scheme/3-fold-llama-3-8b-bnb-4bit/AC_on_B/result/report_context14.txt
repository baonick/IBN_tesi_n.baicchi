--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The financial provider hftGlobalOp requires a high frequency trading service to manage 500,000 orders. This infrastructure receives stock prices, 
executes decision algorithms, and sends purchase orders seeking the fastest network path. The service chain begins with a <marketFeedVF, 16> allocated at the edge. 
For this component, caching is applied after it. The chain then connects to a <tradingAlgoVF, 32> allocated at the edge, ensuring that it is mandatory for the latency 
from the <marketFeedVF, 16> to this <tradingAlgoVF, 32> to remain below 50 ms. The chain proceeds to a <riskCheckerVF, 16> located in the cloud; it is ideally preferred that the 
bandwidth from the <tradingAlgoVF, 32> to this <riskCheckerVF, 16> remains higher than 5000 mbps. Finally, the system routes to an <orderDispatcherVF, 8> allocated at the edge, 
with a preference that the latency from the <riskCheckerVF, 16> to this <orderDispatcherVF, 8> remains below 5 ms, and ensuring that logging is executed before it.
