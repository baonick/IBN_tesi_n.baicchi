--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The financial provider hftGlobalOp requires a high frequency trading service to manage 500,000 orders. This infrastructure receives stock prices, 
executes decision algorithms, and sends purchase orders seeking the fastest network path. The deployed service chain includes, in order: a <marketFeedVF, 16> allocated at the edge, a <tradingAlgoVF, 32> allocated at the edge, a <riskCheckerVF, 16> located in the cloud, and an <orderDispatcherVF, 8> allocated at the edge. 
Regarding requirements, caching is applied after the <marketFeedVF, 16>. Furthermore, it is strictly required that the latency from the <marketFeedVF, 16> to the 
<tradingAlgoVF, 32> remains below 50 ms. The provider prefers that the bandwidth from the <tradingAlgoVF, 32> to the <riskCheckerVF, 16> remains higher than 5000 mbps. 
Additionally, it is desired that the latency from the <riskCheckerVF, 16> to the <orderDispatcherVF, 8> remains below 5 ms. Finally, logging is executed before 
the <orderDispatcherVF, 8>.
