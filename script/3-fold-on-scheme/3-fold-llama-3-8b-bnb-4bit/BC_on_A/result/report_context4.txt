--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The compliance provider gdprComplianceOp requires a GDPR verification service to manage 5,000 corporate database connections. This service scans 
corporate databases to identify unprotected personal data, applies automatic anonymization, and generates reports for regulators. The deployed service 
chain includes, in order: a <dbScannerVF, 16> allocated at the edge, an <anonymizerVF, 24> in the cloud, and a <reportGeneratorVF, 8> located in the cloud. 
Regarding requirements, privacy must be applied after the <dbScannerVF, 16>. Furthermore, it is desired that the latency from the <dbScannerVF, 16> to the 
<anonymizerVF, 24> remains lower than 20 ms. It is strictly required that the bandwidth from the <anonymizerVF, 24> to the <reportGeneratorVF, 8> is higher than 50 Mbps. Finally, 
security is mandatory before the <reportGeneratorVF, 8>.
