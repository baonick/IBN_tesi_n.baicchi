--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The media provider liveSubsOp requires a live subtitling service to manage 10,000 concurrent streams. This service intercepts the audio of a 
streaming event, transforms it into text via AI, translates it into three languages, and reinserts it into the video feed as subtitles. 
The deployed service chain includes, in order: an <audioExtractorVF, 8> allocated at the edge, a <speechToTextVF, 32> function located in the cloud, 
a <multiTranslatorVF, 24> located in the cloud, and a <subtitleInjectorVF, 12> allocated at the edge. Regarding requirements, caching is applied after 
the <audioExtractorVF, 8>. Furthermore, the provider prefers that the total chain hardware remains lower than 100 gb. Finally, it is strictly 
required that the latency from the <speechToTextVF, 32> function to the <subtitleInjectorVF, 12> remains below 20 ms.
