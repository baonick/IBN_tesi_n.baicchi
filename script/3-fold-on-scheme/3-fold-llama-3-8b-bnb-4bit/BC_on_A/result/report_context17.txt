--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The logistics provider deliveryLogisticsOp requires a route optimization service to manage 15,000 couriers. This software receives the GPS positions of a fleet, 
analyzes city traffic data, constantly recalculates the best route for each courier, and updates their terminals. The deployed service chain includes, in order: 
a <gpsIngesterVF, 8> allocated at the edge, a <trafficAnalyzerVF, 24> located in the cloud, a <routeOptimizerVF, 32> located in the cloud, and a <terminalUpdaterVF, 12> allocated at the edge. 
Regarding requirements, compression is applied after the <gpsIngesterVF, 8>. Furthermore, decoding is executed before the <terminalUpdaterVF, 12>. Finally, it is strictly required 
that the latency from the begin to the end of the chain remains below 80 ms.
