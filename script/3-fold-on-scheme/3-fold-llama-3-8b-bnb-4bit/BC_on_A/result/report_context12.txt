--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The enterprise provider corpDocOp requires a document classification engine to manage 50,000 daily files. This system analyzes PDF files or 
OCR scans, identifies the document type like invoice, contract, or reminder, and routes it to the competent business function. 
The deployed service chain includes, in order: an <ocrScannerVF, 16> allocated at the edge, a <textExtractorVF, 12> located in the edge, an <aiDocumentClassifierVF, 32> located in the cloud, a <dataRedactorVF, 16> located in the cloud, and adocumentRouterVF located in the cloud. Regarding requirements, 
security is applied after the <ocrScannerVF, 16>. Furthermore, it is a mandatory requirement that the <ocrScannerVF, 16> and the <textExtractorVF, 12> are deployed 
on the same hosting node. It is strictly required that the latency from the <textExtractorVF, 12> to the <aiDocumentClassifierVF, 32> remains below 50 ms. 
Additionally, privacy is executed before the <dataRedactorVF, 16>. Finally, the provider prefers that the bandwidth from the <aiDocumentClassifierVF, 32> to 
the <documentRouterVF, 8> remains higher than 500 mbps.
