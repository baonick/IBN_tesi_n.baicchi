--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The cybersecurity provider secureMailOp requires a service to manage 10,000 corporate mailboxes. This pipeline intercepts incoming emails, 
removes phishing links, analyzes attachments in an isolated environment, and delivers only safe messages to the server. The service chain includes, 
in order: a <mailGatewayVF, 4> allocated at the edge, a <phishingFilterVF, 8> located in the cloud, a <sandboxScannerVF, 16> located in the cloud, and a <safeDeliveryVF, 4> 
node located in the cloud. Regarding requirements, decoding must be applied after the <mailGatewayVF, 4>. Furthermore, the provider prefers to keep the 
latency from the <mailGatewayVF, 4> to the <phishingFilterVF, 8> below 50 milliseconds. It is strictly required that the bandwidth from the <phishingFilterVF, 8> to 
the <sandboxScannerVF, 16> is higher than 100 Mbps. Finally, logging is mandatory before the <sandboxScannerVF, 16>.
