--- INTENTO TESTUALE ---
The digital provider govDataOp requires an open data portal service to manage 30,000 dataset downloads per day. This system extracts datasets from public databases, 
normalizes them into open formats, adds search metadata, and makes them available for public download. The service chain begins with a <dataExtractorVF, 8> 
allocated at the edge. The chain then connects to a <formatNormalizerVF, 16> located in the cloud, specifying that data caching is applied after it. 
The next component is a <metadataEnricherVF, 12> located in the cloud; it is mandatory that this <formatNormalizerVF, 16> and the <metadataEnricherVF, 12> are hosted 
together on the exact same physical server, while preferring that the network bandwidth between them remains higher than 1000 mbps. Finally, 
the system routes the files to a <downloadProviderVF, 16> located in the cloud, ensuring that the total latency from the initial <dataExtractorVF, 8> to 
this final <downloadProviderVF, 16> is strictly required to remain below 200 ms.
