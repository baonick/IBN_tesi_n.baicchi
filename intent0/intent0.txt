The energy provider requires a service to manage 20,000 smart meters. The service chain includes, in order: a meter gateway allocated at the edge, a load analyzer allocated at the edge, and a grid database located in the cloud. Regarding requirements, input data to the load analyzer Mandatory undergoes caching. It is a mandatory requirement that the latency from the gateway to the analyzer remains below 20 milliseconds. Furthermore, it is preferred that the gateway and the analyzer operate on the same node.
