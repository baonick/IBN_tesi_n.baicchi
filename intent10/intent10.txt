The city operator needs to manage traffic for 50000 connected vehicles. The service chain is composed, in order, of a vehicular receiver at the edge, a context analyzer at the edge, a route optimizer at the edge, and a traffic database in the cloud. Moving to the requirements, the data requires compression before reaching the route optimizer. It is mandatory that the latency from the network gateway to the route optimizer is less than 15 milliseconds. It is Preferred to maintain the total hardware utilization of the chain below 100 gigabytes.
