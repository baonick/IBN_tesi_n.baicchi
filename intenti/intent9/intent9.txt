The healthcare facility healthOp tracks the parameters of 15000 elderly patients. The sequence of network functions 
includes a vital signs aggregator at the edge, an anomaly detector at the edge, and a historical patient 
database in the cloud. Moving to the specifications, the system requires the application of caching before 
the data reaches the anomaly detector. It is mandatory that the latency from the network gateway to the detector 
is less than 10 milliseconds. Furthermore, it is Mandatory that the aggregator and the detector physically reside 
on the same node
