The large farm manages the work of 250 autonomous tractors. The VNF sequence includes a position receiver at the edge, a soil analyzer at the edge, a trajectory planner at the edge, a collision manager in the cloud, and an agricultural database in the cloud. Moving to the rules, the navigation data directed to the trajectory planner requires a security function. It is mandatory that the total hardware resource usage of the chain remains under 128 gigabytes. It is preferred that the latency from the network gateway to the trajectory planner   is less than 15 milliseconds.
