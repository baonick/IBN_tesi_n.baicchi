The oil company monitors 5000 acoustic sensors. The necessary network functions in order are only an acoustic aggregator allocated at the edge and a leak analyzer located in the cloud. For the transformation expectations, the raw data requires prior compression before entering the leak analyzer. It is Preferred to maintain a latency from the network gateway to the leak analyzer below 40 milliseconds. On the contrary, it is mandatory that the total hardware consumption does not exceed 32 gigabytes.
