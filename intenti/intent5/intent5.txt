The logistics company logisticsOp launches a service for 800 delivery drones. The required functions in order are a flight 
receiver at the edge, a route optimizer at the edge, and a shipping archive in the cloud. Regarding the 
constraints, the data sent to the route optimizer requires prior compression. It is mandatory that the service 
availability is higher than 99.9%. It is preferred to assign a dedicated edge node to the route optimizer to 
avoid processing interference.
