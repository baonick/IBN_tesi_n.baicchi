The port authority requires a system to track 50000 containers in transit. The architectural chain is composed, in order, of a signal aggregator located at the edge and a tracking engine allocated in the cloud. Regarding the operational specifications, the updates generated by the tracking engine are recorded in the cloud via logging. It is mandatory to ensure a bandwidth from the network gateway to the tracking engine higher than 200 megabits per second. Furthermore, it is preferred that the signal aggregator operates in isolation on a dedicated edge node.
