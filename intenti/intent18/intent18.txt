The agricultural cooperative farmCoopOp collects data from 1500 weather stations. The architectural chain consists solely of 
a weather collector located at the edge and a prediction engine hosted in the cloud. For the analysis history, the 
activity of the prediction engine is recorded in the cloud via logging. It is mandatory that the total hardware of 
the chain does not exceed 50 gigabytes. It is preferred to have a bandwidth from the weather collector to the 
prediction engine higher than 50 megabits per second.
