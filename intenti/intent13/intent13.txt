The gas provider manages energy for 100000 homes. The sequence of functions includes a home aggregator at the edge, a consumption analyzer at the edge, a billing engine in the cloud, and a customer database in the cloud. To protect users, privacy policies are applied before entering the billing engine. All transactions of the billing engine are recorded in the cloud via logging. It is mandatory to have a bandwidth from the billing engine to the  customer database higher than 100 megabits per second.
