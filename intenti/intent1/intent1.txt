The university campus universityOp installs a biometric access control system for 5000 students. 
The architectural chain starts with an access terminal at the edge, followed by a biometric analyzer at the edge, 
an access controller at the edge, and ends with a campus database in the cloud. Regarding performance, 
the data entering the biometric analyzer requires a security function. It is mandatory that the latency 
from the network gateway to the access controller is less than 10 milliseconds to ensure the instantaneous 
opening of the turnstiles. It is Preferred that the total hardware resource usage of the chain remains below 
64 gigabytes.
